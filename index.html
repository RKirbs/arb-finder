4. Your sportsbooks

Tick only books you can actually bet at. Every 10 books costs 1 credit per market, per sport (or per game for props).

How it works. For every game, the finder takes the best price on each side of a market across the books you selected. If backing every side at those prices costs less than what any single result pays back, the line is an arbitrage. The stakes shown split your bankroll so every outcome pays the same.

Before you bet. Odds move fast. Confirm every price on the sportsbook before placing a leg, and place the harder-to-get leg first. Books can limit accounts that arb, void bets on obvious errors, and apply different rules to things like overtime or postponements. Exchange fees are not included. Nothing here is betting advice.
